04  Coastal windstorm

WPI-8 on the Texas coast

WPI-8 and WPI-8E certificates are required for windstorm insurance in the fourteen first tier coastal counties of Texas. These certifications must be performed by licensed engineers. Coastal windstorm work is a core practice area for the firm at launch.
