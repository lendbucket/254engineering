04 · Coastal windstorm

Windstorm certification on the Texas coast

WPI-8 and WPI-8E certificates are required for windstorm insurance in the fourteen first tier coastal counties of Texas. These certifications must be performed by licensed engineers. Coastal windstorm work will be a core practice area of the firm at launch.
