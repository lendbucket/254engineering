## THE TRAP, AND IT IS A REAL ONE

Do not target "foundation certification" on any page. That term is owned by ITIL, PRINCE2 and other IT certification courses: the top twenty matching terms are itil foundation certification at 3,500, prince2 foundation certification at 400, and so on. Intent is entirely wrong and the page would never rank for a buyer.

The commercial term for that service is foundation inspection, 7,900 at KD 24 with a $12.00 CPC, or structural engineer foundation inspection, 700 at KD 0 with a $7.00 CPC. Rename the service page accordingly. The service itself is still a foundation certification letter; the page is about foundation inspection.

## WHAT THE SITE SHOULD LEAD WITH

The home page H1 currently reads "Texas Engineering Certifications, Sealed and Delivered". Change it to lead with the structural engineer term, since that is where the demand is and where the firm is strongest. Propose the exact H1; it must contain "structural engineer" and "Texas", read as a sentence a person would say, and not be stuffed.

## HOW EACH PAGE IS OPTIMISED

- The H1 leads with the primary term, as a sentence rather than a keyword string.
- The title tag contains the primary term and the location where one applies, under 60 characters.
- The meta description contains the primary term and a reason to click, under 155 characters.
- The primary term appears in the first paragraph, naturally.
- Supporting terms appear as H2s where they are questions people actually ask.
- Every page links to the pillar and the pillar links to every page.
- Schema: Service or ProfessionalService, with the price where one is published, and FAQPage where a page answers questions.
- No keyword stuffing, and no sentence written for a crawler rather than a reader. A page that ranks and does not convert is worth nothing.

## WHAT TO CHECK BEFORE WRITING

Read the existing pages and the shared keyword registry first, and report which of these eleven targets is already served, by which page, and whether that page is targeting the right term today. Where two pages would compete for one term, say so and propose which keeps it. Cannibalisation is the most likely defect in this pass.

Report the mapping before you write a word of copy.
