| Option | Means | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | The element is acceptable with no conditions | A pass |
| B | Acceptable only with a condition: a load limit, or something the contractor must do | A pass, and the condition is listed in the letter's schedule |
| C | Repairs required | Not a pass |

because the sentence promises "the conditions in the attached schedule".

`src/lib/structural-letter-verdict.ts` (the rule; the engineer chooses A or B on
a pass, and the platform never infers it), and
`scripts/proofs/the-permit-letter-takes-its-option.mjs`, which pins all three,
B with an empty schedule, and each sentence against his Word file.
