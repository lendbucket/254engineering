## How the list was made, and what it does not have

- **One rule, from the ruling:** pages only for protocols the engineer has
  SIGNED. On production that is MH, SL, PL, RS and DS v1.1 (the counterpart's
  sitting of 2026-10-10). RC-001 is listed separately, waiting on Robert's
  confirmation of its live status. WS-001 and WP-001 are not signed and have no
  rows; FC-001 v1.0 is staged with migration 0069 and has none either.
- **Each question is one the signed document actually answers**, anchored to
  the part of it that answers it: its pass criteria, its checklist sections, or
  its other outcomes. A page answers from the document; it never says more than
  the document does.
- **No volumes and no difficulty.** Those come from an Ahrefs pull, which is
  run from chat with its cost stated and Robert's approval, or from Search
  Console figures he supplies. The columns are blank on purpose rather than
  guessed.
- **Overlap checked against this site:** each line has a service page at
  `/services/<slug>`, and the four insight articles are on registration,
  procurement, the engineer of record and the PE licence lookup. A question
  page must not restate its service page; it answers one question in depth and
  links to the service page once, in prose.
- **The differentiation record comes first.** `data/keyword-registry.ts` gives
  each brand's angle per topic; homeowner education is where Sealed Engineering
  writes too. Each approved row is checked there before writing, and written
  from the document for this brand's reader, the procurement and institutional
  one.
- **The gate.** Pages are written in the abstract ("a certification checks..."),
  never "we certify", while the gate is shut; voice-audit enforces it.
