**H1: Roof Certification in Texas, Sealed by a Licensed Engineer**

**$549. Sealed letter from a licensed Texas professional engineer.**

### What it is

A roof certification is a letter from a licensed engineer stating the condition of an existing roof as of the date it was inspected. Carriers use it to bind or renew coverage. Lenders and title companies use it to close a sale. Some buyers ask for one before removing an option.

It is not a repair estimate, a warranty, or a promise the roof will not leak. It is a professional opinion about what the roof looked like when a qualified person examined it, issued by an engineer who put his seal on it.

### When you need one

Your insurer will not renew without one.
You are selling and the buyer's lender is asking.
Your roof is over a certain age and the carrier wants an opinion before binding.
You were declined and want a defensible second opinion.

### What it costs

$549 for a standard residential roof, single structure, walkable slope.

What changes it: a roof that cannot be safely walked and needs lift or drone access, a property outside our standard service area, or a return visit after repairs. We tell you the number before we take the job, not after.

### What we will not do

We do not certify a roof we did not inspect.

### Questions people ask

**My roof failed. What now?**
You get a written repair list: every item, what governs it, and what closes it. Repairs are done by your contractor. When they are finished we come back, verify each item individually, and issue the letter. You pay for the return visit, not for a second full inspection.

**Can you just look at photos I already have?**
No. Our engineer's protocol requires evidence collected by our own technician, timestamped and located by our app. Photographs from other devices are not accepted. That is what makes the letter defensible.
