## THE HEADLINE: CANNIBALISATION IS ALREADY LIVE, BEFORE ANY NEW PAGE IS BUILT

`/structural-engineer` is the pillar, and **it is targeting target 5 rather than
target 1 today.**

Those are target 5's terms, and target 5 asks for a **separate page** at

**It is worse than two.** `/structural-engineer/when-you-need-one` already

"when do I need a structural engineer" currently has **two** pages competing, and
target 5 would make three.

**Proposal.** The pillar is retargeted to the commercial term and the
informational angle moves out of it, which is what the revision asks for anyway:

- `/structural-engineer` leads with "structural engineer" as a Texas firm page.
  Commercial intent, 18,000 a month at KD 0.
- `/what-is-a-structural-engineer` takes the informational terms, 1,900 a month.
- `/structural-engineer/when-you-need-one` keeps "when you need one" and is
  linked from both. It is the narrowest of the three and the only one that
  should keep that phrasing.

**The pillar's H1 must change, and that is the single highest value edit in this
pass.** The current one gives an 18,000 a month commercial term to a page that
opens by defining the job.

## THE CONFLICT WITH STANDING LAW, WHICH I HAVE NOT RESOLVED

**Target 11 asks each coverage page to lead with "structural engineer <city>".
CLAUDE.md section 5 forbids city geo pages outright.**

The standing law is explicit: geo on this brand is REGIONAL, the shipped pages
are the eight coverage regions, "there are no city geo pages and no
service-times-place combo pages, which is the doorway trap", and
`/corpus-christi` is the single deliberate exception because it is where the firm
actually is.

The coverage pages are **regions**, not cities: Coastal Bend, Greater Houston,
Dallas Fort Worth, San Antonio, Austin Central Texas, Rio Grande Valley, West
Texas, Panhandle. "structural engineer houston" is a city term, and Greater
Houston is a region containing it.

**CLAUDE.md outranks a build prompt, so I have not minted city pages.** What I
propose instead:

1. Each **region** page may lead with "structural engineer" plus the region's own
   name, which is honest and is not a doorway, because those pages already carry
   substance that could not be find-and-replaced.
2. `/corpus-christi` may honestly take "structural engineer corpus christi",
   because the firm is there. It is the one place a city term is not a doorway.
3. Houston, Dallas, San Antonio and Austin city pages are **not built**, and that
   is a ruling for the operator rather than a decision I take on my own.
