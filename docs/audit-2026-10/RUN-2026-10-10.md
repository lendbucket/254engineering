## Item 4 is blocked, and why

Signing cannot publish the five signed templates (MH, SL, PL, RS, DS v1.1) as
built, for a reason in the schema rather than in the code:

- A template reaches `published` only with its checklist items (0052: a
  deferred check at commit, whatever the door), because dispatch and
  certification run a protocol by its items.
- The five are verbatim TEXT transcriptions (`src/content/protocols/received.ts`):
  no item keys, `BLANK_JOB_LIST_ITEMS = 0`. Only RC-001 is structured, in
  `PROTOCOL_ENTRIES`.
- The repository's own rule: a protocol moves from the received list into
  `PROTOCOL_ENTRIES` "in the same change that records his signature". That move
  puts its intake questions on the order forms, its checklist in front of the
  technician and its determinations in front of the engineer.

**The question.** Options, recommendation first:

1. **Structure each signed protocol, one branch each, then publish.** Parse
   each document's Appendix A (intake), B (checklist) and C (decisions) into the
   registry's structure, proved word for word as RC-001's is; then the signing
   function publishes with those items, and the migration backfills the five
   from their seal acts. Most work; it is what "signed and in force" means.
2. Publish with an empty checklist: refused by the schema, and would loosen a
   check. Not offered.
3. Leave them signed and unpublished until each is structured: the readiness
   table's current state.
